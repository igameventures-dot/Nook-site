FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?▾
Browse products, tap Add to Cart, go to Cart, fill your name, phone and address, then tap Place Order & Pay Now.
How do I pay?▾
We accept bank transfer to our OPay account shown at checkout — 9079478180 (Oladimeji Peace Olarewaju). Transfer the exact amount and tap “I Have Transferred”. We confirm and deliver.
How long does delivery take?▾
Delivery time depends on your location in Ibadan and product availability. We will confirm your delivery date on WhatsApp after your payment is approved.
Can I cancel my order?▾
Yes, you can cancel an order that is still “Pay Now” / unpaid. Once paid or awaiting approval, you cannot cancel from the app — contact us on WhatsApp.
What if I already transferred but it says Awaiting Approval?▾
That means we have received your report. Our admin will verify the transfer and approve it. You will see it change to Paid. Message us on WhatsApp if it takes too long.
Do you deliver outside Ibadan?▾
Yes, we can arrange delivery outside Ibadan. Delivery fee depends on location. Chat with us on WhatsApp before ordering for a quote.
Still need help?

Message us and we will reply quickly.
